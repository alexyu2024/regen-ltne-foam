# Real-gas helium in ltneFoam

The default gas model of every case is the ideal gas. This page describes the optional real-gas helium model, its
data and validation, and its effect on the L5 regenerator slice. Bars and results: `docs/VERIFICATION.md`, levels
LP and LRG; `results/LP`, `results/LRG`, `results/LRG_A3`.

## Model

The library `src/ltneRealGas` registers one thermo combination for `heRhoThermo`:

    heRhoThermo<pureMixture<tabulated2D<species::thermo<hConst<tabulatedRealGas<specie>>, sensibleEnthalpy>>>>

- **Equation of state `tabulatedRealGas`**: it returns rho(p, T) and the real-gas departures that OpenFOAM's thermo
  adds to the ideal-gas part.
  - Enthalpy departure: h(T, p) - h(T, p -> 0). Also c_p, c_v (real minus ideal) and the entropy departure.
  - psi = rho/p and Z = p/(rho R T), as `PengRobinsonGas` does; c_p - c_v comes from the table.
- **Ideal-gas part `hConst`**: Cp = cp0 = 2.5 R. This is exact for a monatomic gas, with R from CoolProp's molar mass
  4.002602 kg/kmol. The equation of state stops the run if `molWeight` or `Cp` differ from the table's values.
- **Transport `tabulated2D`**: mu(p, T) and kappa(p, T); alphah = kappa / c_p with the real-gas c_p.
- **Interpolation**: bilinear on a uniform T-p grid. A state outside the table is a fatal error; nothing is
  extrapolated or clamped (LP.17-LP.18).
- **Loading**: the library is loaded with `libs (ltneRealGas);` in `system/controlDict`. The solver is not relinked,
  and time stepping and the solve sequence are unchanged.
- **Enthalpy monitors**: the ⟨H⟩ monitors use h relative to 0 K. With a real gas they need the ideal-gas part,
  `enthalpyReferenceCp` = cp0 in `constant/ltneProperties`. Without the entry the ideal-gas behaviour is unchanged.

## Data

`src/ltneRealGas/tables/helium_CoolProp.dat` is generated by `tools/heliumRealGas/genHeliumTable.py`:
- **Source**: CoolProp 8.0.0 (MIT licence; `requirements.txt` pins it).
  - Equation of state: Ortiz-Vega et al., J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data (2019).
  - Viscosity: Arp (NIST 1998). Conductivity: Hands (Cryogenics 1981).
  - At the LP check points CoolProp agrees with the NIST Chemistry WebBook to every printed digit.
- **Range**: 15-95 K and 0.5-1.0 MPa, in steps of 0.25 K and 0.025 MPa (321 x 21 nodes, 765 KB).
  - At its periodic state the L5 slice runs at about 22-84 K, and its pressure swings between about 0.74 and 0.87
    MPa around a mean of 0.80 MPa.
  - CoolProp's helium viscosity has a jump near 100 K, so the table stops at 95 K.
- **Interpolation error**: the largest bilinear error at the cell centres, against CoolProp, is 1.2e-4 for rho and
  2.9e-5 for c_p, both at the 15 K corner (written in the file header).

## Validation (level LP, in CI)

`realGasPropsCheck` builds OpenFOAM's own thermo object and evaluates it at six NIST points:
- 22, 50 and 80 K at 0.75 MPa (table nodes);
- 22.125, 50.125 and 80.125 K at 0.7625 MPa (cell centres, where bilinear error is largest).

The bar is a relative difference of at most 0.1 % for density and c_p.

| point | rho, relative difference | c_p, relative difference |
|---|---|---|
| 22 / 50 / 80 K, 0.75 MPa | 1.8e-7 / 5.6e-8 / 6.8e-8 | 8.9e-9 / 2.6e-8 / 5.9e-9 |
| 22.125 / 50.125 / 80.125 K, 0.7625 MPa | 3.6e-5 / 1.8e-6 / 1.0e-6 | 5.2e-6 / 1.1e-7 / 6.6e-8 |

- **Round trip**: the h -> T round trip through OpenFOAM's Newton iteration is exact to 7e-8 K.
- **Transport**: mu and kappa at 50 K agree with NIST to 1e-7.
- **Out-of-table runs**: four runs just outside the table all stop with the out-of-table error.

How different is helium from an ideal gas here? At 0.75 MPa the real-gas c_p is 5503 J/kg/K at 22 K, 6 % above the
ideal 5193; at 80 K it is 5215 (+0.4 %). The density at the same p and T differs from p/(RT) by +0.04 % at 22 K and
-1.2 % at 80 K.

## Using it

    python3 cases/L5_regenSlice/mkCase.py <case> --gas real

This writes the real-gas `thermophysicalProperties`, copies the table, sets `enthalpyReferenceCp` and adds the
library to the case's controlDict. Without `--gas real` the output is the ideal-gas case, byte for byte. For other
cases, copy `cases/LP_heliumRealGas/constant/thermophysicalProperties` and the table, and add the `libs` line.

## Effect on the L5 slice (level LRG)

**Method.**
- **Ideal-gas side**: the accepted ideal-gas periodic reference of L5P; no new ideal-gas run.
- **Real-gas side**: the same case continued from that reference with only the gas model switched. It is then run
  to its own periodic state under the same quantities, tolerances and acceptance rule (L5P Amendment A2).
- **Time scale**: the slow matrix mode has a time constant of about 820 cycles (r = 0.99878), so the real-gas run
  needs several thousand cycles.

**Charge basis: equal initial mean pressure.** At the switch, p and T are the reference's.
- The mean pressure there is about 0.80 MPa, not the nominal 0.75 MPa; the sealed column settles there.
- At the same p and T the real gas holds less helium: -0.72 % in the dry run, mostly in the warm half of the
  matrix. The value of the actual run is LRG.2.
- Equal mass would need a mean pressure about 0.7 % higher. That run was not made.
- Every relative difference below holds at equal initial mean pressure.

**Results** (`results/LRG_A3`, equal initial mean pressure):

| quantity | ideal gas (cycle 4900) | real gas (cycle 11100) | relative difference | uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q_hot, heat from the hot HX, W | -12.1892 | -12.7704 | +4.77 % | 0.011 % |
| Q_cold, heat from the cold HX, W | 1.85456 | 1.97519 | +6.50 % | 0.005 % |
| p_mean, MPa | 0.80252 | 0.81206 | +1.19 % (drift) | - |
| helium inventory at the switch | M_ideal | M_real0 | -0.72 % | - |

- **Outcome.** The real-gas run did not pass the A2 test by the registered cap, N_ref + 6000 = cycle 10900
  (max R_q/tol 0.271 against 0.2; `results/LRG`, LRG.4 FAIL). Under Amendment A3, registered before that result,
  the cap was raised to cycle 12900 and the run continued; it passed at cycle 11100 (max R_q/tol 0.193;
  `results/LRG_A3`, 5/5 PASS). The values at the two cycles differ by less than 0.002 percentage points.
- **Reading.** Both HX heat flows grow in magnitude with the real gas, the cold one more. The mean pressure rises
  1.2 % above the reference although the helium mass is fixed (LRG.5, drift 5e-11). The differences are 400-1300
  times their uncertainties. They hold for this charge basis: at equal mass the real-gas mean pressure would be
  about 0.7 % higher again, and that run was not made.

The uncertainty is the sum of the two runs' remaining distances to their periodic states (A2 rule), relative to the
ideal-gas value.

## Limits

- The table covers helium only, 15-95 K and 0.5-1.0 MPa; outside that range the run stops.
- Bilinear interpolation makes h and c_p consistent only to the table's resolution (round trip 7e-8 K).
- The pressure equation uses psi = rho/p, the secant, as `PengRobinsonGas` does, not the tangent (d rho/d p)_T;
  the converged state uses the table's rho.
- Cases are 1-D and serial. No comparison with Fluent or with experiments is included.
