# L5P-X1 registration: Graves-Morris m = 3, budget 1280 (2026-10-25)

Registered and pushed before the run starts. No criterion below is changed after any number of this run is read.
Ordered by leader-gen58 on 2026-10-25, after the seven L5P configurations all FAILed under the registered rules.

## What is run

- Method: (c) Graves-Morris, `pss/methods.py: graves_morris`, m = 3 plain cycles per round, unchanged from the L5P
  registration (`docs/VERIFICATION.md`, L5P and Amendment A1).
- Start: the cycle-40 state of the natural run (`cases/L5P_periodicState/run_c40`, recreated with
  `L5P_RUN=run_c40 L5P_CYCLES=40 ./runNatural.sh` if missing). The 40 cycles are counted.
- Solver: `ltneFoam` as on branch `pss/conclusion` (main at eab2432), OpenFOAM `openfoam2512-dev` 2512.0-2.

## What changes against L5P

Only the budget.
- L5P: a method not ended within 600 cycles after cycle 40 is FAIL at 640.
- **Here: a method not ended within 1240 cycles after cycle 40 is FAIL at a total of 1280** (`L5P_BUDGET=1240`).
  The leader's "budget of 1280 cycles" is read as the total honest count, as 640 was in L5P.

## Unchanged

- Stop rule (L5P with A1): correction <= 0.005 K and residual Phi(x) - x <= 1.5e-5 K, max norm; corrections capped
  at 2 K.
- Verification: 50 natural cycles after the stop, the first 10 untested; D, NR and the method's own drift test with
  r_A2 = 0.998813 and drift tolerance 0.45 tol (1 - r_A2) per cycle (Amendment A2). A failed self-test continues
  the iteration; its cycles are counted and stay inside the budget.
- Pass (V test against the reference): E, D, NR and M as in L5P, against `results/L5P/reference.json` (cycle 4900,
  accepted under A2). Tolerances: Q_hot 0.005 W, Q_cold 0.0005 W, dT_seg1..4, Ts_seg1..4 mean and T_joint 0.02 K,
  p_mean 1e-3 relative; mass 1e-9.
- Honest count: total = 40 + every map evaluation, verification included.
- Comparison: natural marching, 4195 cycles. A PASS with total < 4195 is an acceleration; a PASS with total >= 4195
  is not.

## Outcome rules

- PASS or FAIL as above, scored by `python3 -m pss.study score`. No amendment of this registration. On FAIL the
  next pending configuration is L5P-X2 (Newton-Picard with a larger subspace), registered in its own dated file
  before it runs.
- The first 600 cycles after cycle 40 replay the L5P gm3 run of the same method. They are expected to be bit for bit
  the same as `results/L5P/methods/gm3_evals.csv`. That is a determinism check, not a criterion.

## Output

`results/L5P/accel/X1_gm3_b1280/`: `gm3_evals.csv` (written as it grows), `gm3_hist.csv`, `gm3_verify.csv`,
`gm3_summary.json`, and the copied `reference.json`.
