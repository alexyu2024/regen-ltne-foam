# L5P-X1c registration: Graves-Morris m = 3, corrected cycle mean, start at cycle 400 (2026-10-27)

Registered and pushed before any cycle of this configuration runs, including the natural cycles to the start state.
- Approved by the author (Qiming Yu) on 2026-10-27 as one robustness check of the X1b PASS.
- This is the last configuration: whatever its result, no further run follows. Newton-Picard (X2) and BRR (X3) are
  not run.

## Criteria: those of X1b, unchanged

Copied from `docs/L5P_accel/2026-10-25_gm3_b1280_fixedmean.md` (via `docs/L5P_accel/2026-10-25_gm3_b1280.md`):
- **Method**: (c) Graves-Morris, `pss/methods.py: graves_morris`, m = 3, with the corrected cycle-mean driver
  (`restartOnRestart`, 2400-step check).
- **Budget**: 1240 cycles after the start state (`L5P_BUDGET=1240`); not ended within it is FAIL.
- **Stop rule**: correction <= 0.005 K and residual Phi(x) - x <= 1.5e-5 K, max norm; corrections capped at 2 K.
- **Verification**: 50 natural cycles after the stop, the first 10 untested. The method's own drift test uses
  r_A2 = 0.998813 and drift tolerance 0.45 tol (1 - r_A2) per cycle. A failed self-test continues the iteration;
  its cycles are counted and stay inside the budget.
- **Pass**: E, D, NR and M against `results/L5P/reference.json` (cycle 4900, A2), scored by
  `python3 -m pss.study score`. Tolerances: Q_hot 0.005 W, Q_cold 0.0005 W, dT_seg1..4, Ts_seg1..4 mean and
  T_joint 0.02 K, p_mean 1e-3 relative; mass 1e-9.
- **Comparison**: natural marching, 4195 cycles. A PASS with total < 4195 is an acceleration.

## The only change: the start state

- **Start**: the natural state at cycle 400 instead of 40 (`L5P_START_CYCLE=400`).
- **How it is made**: a copy of `run_c40` (cycle 40 of the natural run, unchanged), continued by natural marching
  for 360 cycles in one `ltneFoam` run: `L5P_RUN=run_c400 ./runNatural.sh <dir> 400 360`.
- **Expected, not a criterion**: its per-cycle table agrees with `results/L5P/natural/per_cycle.csv` for cycles
  41-400 to within the restart noise of the map (1.7e-7 K). It is not bit for bit, because the natural run
  restarted at other cycles.
- **Honest count**: total = 400 + every map evaluation, verification included. The 400 natural cycles to the start
  state are counted in full.
- **Budget in totals**: the budget after the start is X1b's 1240, so a non-ending run is FAIL at a total of 1640.

## Outcome

- PASS or FAIL, reported as it comes out, in `results/L5P/accel/X1c_gm3_c400/README.md`. No amendment.
- Then stop.
